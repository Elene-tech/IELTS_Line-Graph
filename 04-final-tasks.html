Завдання 1 · Electricity from renewable sources, 2000–2025

The line graph below shows the percentage of electricity generated from renewable sources in three countries between 2000 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Крок 2 · Чек-лист перед моделлю (усі 8 пунктів — чесно)

Модель Band 8 · порівнюй рішення, а не слова

§1 The line graph compares the proportion of electricity generated from renewable sources in Germany, the UK and Poland over a 25-year period from 2000 to 2025.

§2 Overall, all three countries became considerably more reliant on renewables, with Germany and the UK converging at similar levels by the end of the period, while Poland lagged behind throughout.

§3 Germany's share rose steadily from 6% in 2000 to 17% in 2010, before climbing sharply to peak at 47% in 2025. The UK followed a similar trajectory from a lower base: after remaining almost flat at around 3–7% for a decade, the figure surged to 40% in 2020 and nearly drew level with Germany, at 45%.

§4 In contrast, Poland saw only modest growth, edging up from 2% to 7% over the first decade and reaching just under 20% by 2025 — less than half the level recorded in the other two countries.

Три рішення для звірки: чи згрупував ти Germany + UK разом? Чи є хоча б один множник чи точне порівняння? Чи «In contrast» відкриває §4?

Завдання 2 · International students, three Canadian universities, 2015–2025

The graph below shows the number of international students enrolled at three Canadian universities between 2015 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Крок 2 · Чек-лист перед моделлю

Модель Band 8 · порівнюй рішення, а не слова

§1 The line graph illustrates how many international students were enrolled at three Canadian universities — the University of Toronto, McGill University and the University of British Columbia (UBC) — between 2015 and 2025.

§2 Overall, all three universities saw substantial growth, although numbers dipped in 2020. Toronto consistently had the highest enrolment, while UBC, despite starting as the smallest, recorded the fastest growth.

§3 In 2015, Toronto enrolled 8,200 international students, considerably more than McGill (6,500) and UBC (5,100). All three figures then climbed steadily, with Toronto reaching 11,600 and UBC almost catching up with McGill by 2019. In 2020, enrolment at all three institutions fell slightly, by between 300 and 500 students.

§4 From 2020 onwards, growth resumed and accelerated. UBC's numbers nearly tripled overall, soaring to 13,800 and overtaking McGill, which reached 11,300. Toronto remained the leader, finishing the period at 15,100 international students.

Крок 3 · Перепиши слабкий абзац

Модуль завершено 🎯
