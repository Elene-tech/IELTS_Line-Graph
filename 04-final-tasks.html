Спроба 1 · Water consumption, three cities, 2010–2025

The line graph below shows average daily water consumption per person in three cities between 2010 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

ІнструкціяНижче — три кроки: introduction → overview → body. На кожному кроці спочатку читай англійську підказку, потім пиши свій варіант. Кнопка з взірцем відкриється після твоєї спроби. Коли всі три частини готові — відкриється повна модельна відповідь.

Крок 1 · Introduction

HintParaphrase the task: change «shows» → «compares/illustrates»; name what is measured (average daily water consumption per person, in litres), the three cities and the years. No data, no opinions.

Sample: The line graph compares average daily water consumption per person in three cities between 2010 and 2025, measured in litres.

Крок 2 · Overview

HintTwo stories, no numbers: (1) consumption rose in City A and levelled off in City B, whereas City C fell markedly; (2) as a result, City A overtook City C. Think «who leads + sharpest contrast».

Sample: Overall, consumption rose in City A and levelled off in City B, whereas City C saw a marked decline. As a result, City A overtook City C to become the largest consumer by the end of the period.

Крок 3 · Body 1 + Body 2

Hint · groupingBody 1 = the two rising lines: A (steady climb) and B (growth that plateaus after 2020). Body 2 = City C, the falling line — open with «In contrast, …». Verb + adverb + approximated figure per sentence; «to» for endpoints.

Sample Body 1: In City A, daily use climbed steadily from 140 litres in 2010 to approximately 185 litres in 2025. City B followed a similar, though flatter, trajectory: consumption grew from 160 to 172 litres and then plateaued, remaining unchanged after 2020.

Sample Body 2: In contrast, City C moved in the opposite direction. Usage fell gradually at first, from 205 to 170 litres, before dropping more sharply to just 150 litres by 2025 — the lowest figure on the graph.

§1 The line graph compares average daily water consumption per person in three cities between 2010 and 2025, measured in litres.

§2 Overall, consumption rose in City A and levelled off in City B, whereas City C saw a marked decline. As a result, City A overtook City C to become the largest consumer by the end of the period.

§3 In City A, daily use climbed steadily from 140 litres in 2010 to approximately 185 litres in 2025. City B followed a similar, though flatter, trajectory: consumption grew from 160 to 172 litres and then plateaued, remaining unchanged after 2020.

§4 In contrast, City C moved in the opposite direction. Usage fell gradually at first, from 205 to 170 litres, before dropping more sharply to just 150 litres by 2025 — the lowest figure on the graph.

Порівняй рішення: де ти поставив «In contrast»? Чи є plateaued / overtook? Чи кожна цифра з to/at?

Спроба 2 · Households with internet access, three regions, 2005–2025

The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three regions between 2005 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Єдина підказка · groupingBody 1 = the two leaders (North and South — similar levels, nearly converge by 2025). Body 2 = Rural — the lowest start but the fastest relative growth (×6). Overview: all rose + the gap narrowed.
ІнструкціяПиши всю відповідь одразу (§1–§4, 150+ слів). Без таймера. Коли допишеш — заповни чек-лист: він відкриє модель.
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю

§1 The line graph illustrates changes in the proportion of households with internet access in three regions — North, South and Rural — between 2005 and 2025.

§2 Overall, internet penetration rose substantially in all three regions, and the gap between them narrowed considerably. Rural households, despite starting far lower, recorded the fastest relative growth.

§3 In 2005, 45% of households in the North region had internet access, compared with 28% in the South and only 12% in Rural areas. Over the following decade all three figures climbed steadily, reaching 75%, 63% and 38% respectively by 2015.

§4 Growth then accelerated in the South and Rural regions. By 2025 the South had almost caught up with the North, at 90% against 92%, while the figure for Rural areas had risen more than sixfold, reaching 75%.

Порівняй: чи лежать North + South разом у §3? Чи є respectively? Чи «more than sixfold» замість сухих цифр?

Чек-лист перед моделлю (усі 8 — чесно)

§1 The line graph shows the percentage of electricity generated from coal, natural gas and renewable sources in one country between 2010 and 2025.

§2 Overall, the three sources moved in different directions: renewables rose dramatically and overtook both fossil fuels, while coal declined steadily and gas fluctuated before falling slightly. By 2025 renewables had become the leading source.

§3 In 2010, coal was the dominant source at 45%, with gas at 32% and renewables at just 15%. The pattern then reversed. Renewables climbed steadily to 35% by 2016, overtaking coal, before surging to 55% by 2025 — almost four times their initial level.

§4 In contrast, coal fell consistently, dropping from 45% to just 14%. Gas, meanwhile, fluctuated between 30% and 35% for most of the period before edging down to 26% by the end of the period.

Порівняй рішення: overview без цифр? Перетин названо подією (overtaking), а не набором років? Gas описано через «fluctuated between» — теж історія, не тільки renewables?
